Measured outcomes / sequential image editing

Repeated edits erase most post-hoc watermarks.

Across InstructPix2Pix, SDEdit, and FLUX.2-klein, ordinary text-guided editing sharply reduced detection of watermarks added after image generation. Re-embedding the decoded payload largely preserved detection.

A separate generated-image track shows a different pattern: Gaussian Shading remained detectable through InstructPix2Pix edits, while Tree-Ring did not.

01 / CORE

Post-hoc watermark retention

Detection after four consecutive edits (L4), measured against the watermarked original (L0). The same 90 source images were evaluated with each editor and watermark.

Plain editing pushed detection close to chance.

After four InstructPix2Pix edits, WAM remained detectable on 19% of images and TrustMark on 23%. SDEdit at strength 0.5 reduced all three post-hoc detectors to 0% at L4. FLUX.2-klein likewise left 0% WAM and DWT-DCT-SVD detections and 2% TrustMark detections.

04 / CHECKS

Negative controls and interpretation

No false positives were observed on the unwatermarked tracks under the study's target operating point.

False-positive checks

0 false positives per detector: 1,350 negative rows in stage 2, 90 in the guided test, and 450 per detector in the generated-image track.

What the comparison says

Post-hoc marks are vulnerable to diffusion-based regeneration. A pipeline that decodes the input payload and re-embeds it after editing preserved detection more consistently than plain or guided editing.

05 / METHODS

Study design

Each edit is evaluated sequentially so the reported retention reflects repeated transformations of the same image, not independent one-shot edits.

Detection threshold

One-sided binomial test calibrated to a target false-positive rate of 10-3. Negative controls are reported separately.

Post-hoc track

90 PIE-Bench++ images; WAM, TrustMark, and DWT-DCT-SVD; InstructPix2Pix, SDEdit, and FLUX.2-klein; baseline and decode-edit-re-embed.

Generated-image track

45 generated images with Tree-Ring or Gaussian Shading; InstructPix2Pix and SDEdit; baseline editing only.

06 / LIMITS

Limitations

These results characterize the tested watermark implementations, editors, settings, and image sets.

  • The guided-editing validation uses 18 held-out images; its confidence intervals are wide.
  • Detection rates are not universal robustness guarantees and may change with detector thresholds or edit settings.
  • Generation-time and post-hoc watermark results come from different mechanisms and are reported separately.
  • Re-embedding assumes access to a decoder and encoder capable of recovering and reinserting the payload.
